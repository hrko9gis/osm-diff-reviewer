description=Compare reference data with OpenStreetMap and review the differences as update candidates.
about=Matches a reference layer (e.g. municipal open data) against OpenStreetMap data and classifies the differences: missing, geometry difference, attribute difference, ambiguous and OSM only. Points, lines and polygons are supported, and a version-diff mode compares two releases of the reference data and checks only what changed. Review decisions are kept in a GeoPackage and carried over when matching is run again; pairs that changed since a decision are flagged for recheck. The plugin never edits OSM: candidates are handed over to JOSM (Remote Control) or MapRoulette (GeoJSON or API). A licence record per reference source blocks any export of reference data until its use in OSM is confirmed. Importing external data into OSM may fall under the OSM Import Guidelines; consult your local community first. Japanese translation included.
